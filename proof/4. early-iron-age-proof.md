## Entry Four — Early Iron Age

**Timeframe:** ~1,200 BCE → ~800 BCE  

---

### Epistemic Condition

- Collapse of major Bronze Age palace systems  
- Decentralization of authority in many regions  
- Disruption of long-distance trade networks  
- Reduced bureaucratic continuity  
- Gradual diffusion of iron technology  

---

### Definition of Proof

> **Proof = locally stabilized validation under reduced centralized archival authority.**

---

### Locus of Authority

Regional power centers and oral-traditional continuity.

---

### Medium

- Oral transmission  
- Customary law  
- Surviving inscriptions (less systematized than prior phase)  
- Lineage and tribal memory  

---

### Structural Characteristics

- Fragmented  
- Regionally variable  
- Less bureaucratically centralized  
- Re-grounded in local continuity  

---

### Authority Mechanism

Custom and lineage regulate action.  
Proof compels behavior through recognized tradition and locally maintained authority rather than expansive centralized archives.
