## Entry Three — Bronze Age

**Timeframe:** ~3,300 BCE → ~1,200 BCE  

---

### Epistemic Condition

- Emergence of writing systems  
- Administrative states and palace economies  
- Codified law  
- Long-distance trade networks  
- Early bureaucratic record-keeping  

---

### Definition of Proof

> **Proof = inscribed and externally verifiable validation stabilized through symbolic systems.**

---

### Locus of Authority

The symbolic record (tablet, seal, codified inscription).

---

### Medium

- Writing (cuneiform, hieroglyphic systems)  
- Seals and official markings  
- Legal codes  
- Administrative archives  

---

### Structural Characteristics

- Archival  
- Transferable across distance  
- Less dependent on immediate human presence  
- Increasingly institutional  

---

### Authority Mechanism

Recorded inscription regulates action.  
Proof compels behavior through reference to durable symbolic authority rather than solely to living memory.
