## Entry Two — Agricultural Phase

**Timeframe:** ~10,000 BCE → ~3,300 BCE  

---

### Epistemic Condition

- Sedentary settlements  
- Food surplus and storage  
- Early counting practices (tallies, tokens)  
- Repetitive seasonal cycles  
- Emerging role differentiation  

---

### Definition of Proof

> **Proof = stabilized communal validation sufficient for coordinated continuity.**

---

### Locus of Authority

The settled community.

---

### Medium

- Collective memory  
- Repetition across seasons  
- Material markers (tallies, stored goods)  
- Shared routines  

---

### Structural Characteristics

- Local  
- Repetitive  
- More temporally stable than proto-phase  
- Embedded in settlement continuity  

---

### Authority Mechanism

Communal reinforcement regulates action.  
Proof compels behavior through shared expectation formed by repeated validation within the group.
