## Entry One — Baseline: Proto (Pre-Agricultural)

**Timeframe:** ~300,000 BCE → ~10,000 BCE  

---

### Epistemic Condition

- No writing systems  
- No bureaucracy  
- No codified law  
- No durable external symbolic authority  

---

### Definition of Proof

> **Proof = actionable credibility embedded in the human organism.**

---

### Locus of Authority

Embodied human presence.

---

### Medium

- Memory  
- Direct perception  
- Sincerity signals  
- Reputation  

---

### Structural Characteristics

- Relational  
- Immediate  
- Survival-calibrated  

---

### Authority Mechanism

Trust heuristics regulate action.  
Proof compels behavior only to the degree that the group judges the speaker reliable.
